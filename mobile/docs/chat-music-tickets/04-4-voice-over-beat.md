# 4.4 — Voice note over beat

Extend `VOICE`: optional bed track. Record voice + pick a low-volume backing (catalog 30s preview or bundled royalty-free beats). Mix locally (`expo-audio`) before upload, OR store both refs and mix on playback.

**Licensing:** prefer bundled / royalty-free beds. Apple catalog previews only as a stylistic clip, documented as such — never full tracks.

## Acceptance
- Record voice with a selected bed; playback mixes both; royalty-free beds available without subscription.
